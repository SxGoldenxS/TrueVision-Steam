# Luminous / Asmodeus

Researched 5 November 2026. Skill: **Asmodeus, Lord of Lust** (色欲之王, アスモデウス), Luminous Valentine's Ultimate Skill. Her earlier Unique Skill is **Lust**; Memory End Requiem is a related **art**, not another name for the Ultimate Skill.

## Evidence

- [Official Tensura glossary](https://www.ten-sura.com/keyword): its Hope King entry explicitly compares that skill's authority over life and death to Luminous's Ultimate Skill 色欲之王. Its Memory End Requiem entry associates Luminous's Ultimate Skill and her mythical-grade Night Rose blade. This primary source supports the skill and life/death direction.
- [Official character page](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/luminus): identifies the vampire Demon Lord and provides the character design. The inspected art has silver hair, black layered gothic clothing and crimson/blue eyes. It is a design reference only; the portrait is not bundled into the theme.
- [Asmodeus](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Asmodeus): secondary reference for the English/Japanese skill name, evolution from Lust and authority over life and death.
- [Memory End Requiem](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Memory_End_Requiem): secondary reference describes crimson illumination of her blade; cites LN Volume 11 chapter 5 and Volume 20 chapter 3. Those novel passages were not independently checked here.
- [Life Domination](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Life_Domination): secondary source points to LN Volume 11 chapters 4–5. Its list describes multiple users; abilities of those other users are not assumed to belong to Luminous.
- [Night Rose](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Rose): identifies Luminous's weapon and links an Episode 84 image. Direct retrieval of that image was blocked. The [weapon discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TenseiSlime/comments/1u170h4/luminouss_sword_night_rose_is_underrated/) supplies an inspected [anime-frame repost](https://i.redd.it/4ankew5lr96h1.jpeg) and [manga weapon detail](https://i.redd.it/0pkyb0w09a6h1.jpeg). Those images show the crimson blade, blue rose, asymmetric silver guard, dark grip and pointed pommel; the repost's exact episode/chapter attribution was not independently verified. The theme bundles independently drawn vector artwork, not the reposted images.
- [Anti-magic Mask](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-magic_Mask): secondary reference connects the mask with the hero Chronoa/Chloe, Shizu and Rimuru through its ownership loop. It is not exclusive to Chronoa. [Holy Ark](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_Ark) connects Luminous's sealing magic with Chronoa and cites LN Volume 7 chapter 6 and Volume 11 chapters 4–5; the passages were not independently read here. [Chronoa](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Chronoa) distinguishes the Manas from Chloe's hero identity. The cameo refers to their shared story and the masked hero, not a new Luminous skill.
- Mask appearance: direct retrieval of the wiki's linked anime images was blocked. An accessible [fan-made replica photo](https://www.reddit.com/r/TenseiSlime/comments/u5an1i/3d_printed_and_painted_an_anti_magic_mask/) was inspected for the oval silhouette, paired curled markings, cheek strokes, lower curved band and small central dot. It is a secondary visual aid, not canonical artwork or an exact model sheet. The theme's mask is independently drawn and shaded in the profile's restrained silver-violet palette.

## Interpretation boundary

No verified exact Asmodeus seal or full-screen skill-interface design was established in this research. The tall pointed frame, lace-like silver tracery, paired crimson/blue lights, inward energy threads and central blade are an original fan interpretation. Gothic geometry references her character design; crimson references the cited art; blue references her eye color. Blue is not claimed to be a canon resurrection color. No invented runic alphabet or Raphael ring is used. The English skill cartouche is an original layout.

The source is `src/luminous-art.mjs`; the profile is `src/luminous.css`. It shares Steam layout, accessibility, installed-game markers, notification sizing and motion gating with TrueVision. It does not share the Raphael or Great Sage artwork generators.

Version 1.7.1 extends silver botanical filigree into the upper-left and lower background, adds static crimson/silver atmospheric fields and broad fine energy currents, and adds small gothic corner flourishes to shelves, game sections, menus and the library list. These ornaments are original visual interpretations, not additional canon abilities or an exact Night Rose weapon illustration. Corner art stays at 96px so it does not stretch on wide or narrow panels. It uses background layers, preserving native element positions, pointer behavior and text space. The profile's surface selectors have enough specificity to work when Millennium injects the condition before the common stylesheet.

Version 1.7.2 replaces the earlier abstract blade with a reference-based **Night Rose** centerpiece. Its distinctive blue rose, open asymmetric silver basket, wrapped dark grip, pointed pommel and long crimson blade follow the inspected anime/manga views. The sword is displayed point-down within the existing composition. Perspective, proportions, metallic shading and decorative glow are adapted to the theme; this is not an exact weapon model sheet. The surrounding paired lights and skill-space geometry remain an original Asmodeus interpretation. The blade retains one existing pulse, keeping the total at five grouped effects.

The experimental Chronoa mask/Ark cameo from 1.7.3 was rejected and removed in 1.7.4. The earlier research links are retained as research history; the generated artwork restores the 1.7.2 Night Rose composition and contains no Chronoa mask, frame or caption.

## Motion and integration

Still has the full composition and zero animation nodes. Animated has five grouped effects: two slow light pulses, one flow along existing threads, one small movement of the mote group and one blade pulse. No blur filters, cursor tracking, timers or frame loop are added. The animated SVG is fetched only when this profile is animated and active under the existing focus/fullscreen gate; reduced motion uses the still image. No numeric performance claim is made.

The profile has its own opaque-surface texture and notification badge. Existing toast sizes and footer clearance are retained. Installed games remain bright and heavy with a silver marker, uninstalled entries muted, selected entries separately highlighted. Installation preserves the selected live profile.
