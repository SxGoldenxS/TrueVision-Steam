# Local foundation

Requires the installed **Minimal Dark 6.4.1**, by SaiyajinK.

Upstream: https://github.com/SaiyajinK/Minimal-Dark-for-Steam

The base is referenced through Millennium's local asset server and is not bundled or modified. Its selected options were captured when this theme was built. Rebuild to refresh that selection.

Raphael is independently drawn from the anime abstract manifestation and Episode 36 skill card, retaining the user's original RaphaelBackground.client.luau/TrueVision ornamental vocabulary. Great Sage follows its Episode 35 manifestation and Episode 1 card. Ornamental paths approximate the analytical lettering; they are not claimed as an exact canonical alphabet. No anime screenshot is bundled. Title outlines use Noto Serif JP under the included SIL OFL (GREAT-SAGE-FONT-LICENSE.txt). This is a personal fan theme, not official Tensura artwork.

Luminous / Asmodeus is an original life/death interpretation, with its own artwork generator, gothic texture and notification badge. Skill identity and life/death authority are corroborated by the official Tensura glossary; character appearance by the official character page. Its tracery is not claimed to be a canonical skill seal. See LUMINOUS-REFERENCES.md.
